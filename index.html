Weekly training

Your first training is free

What to bring

Bring what you would wear to the gym: sports shoes, a T-shirt, shorts and plenty of water. A towel and change of clothes are useful if you plan to shower afterwards.

The club can usually lend shorts and a rugby jersey, and sometimes boots if your size is available.

Training is open to players aged 16 and over.

Before you come

Send a quick message so the coaches can plan the session and check available kit sizes.

Decided at the last minute? Come anyway—you are welcome.

The club

Different backgrounds. One team.

Nicosia Rugby Club brings together Cypriots, international residents and university students. Train at any level, play Touch and 7s with Nicosia, or play 15s in the Cyprus championship with Ayia Napa Lions.

Club values: Respect. Integrity. Solidarity. Passion. Discipline.
